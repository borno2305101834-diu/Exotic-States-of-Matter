Driving the trap

A practical tour of the controls. Open the Sandbox alongside this guide and follow along.

01

Set the trap geometry first

Atom Number and Trap Frequency both raise Tc, so decide on those before you touch Temperature — they set the target you're cooling toward. Try the default N = 20,000 and 1.00× frequency to start.

02

Sweep the Temperature slider

Drag it from high to low and watch the Trap Chamber: the cloud of orange/gold thermal atoms should contract and a dense cyan core should appear as you cross Tc.

03

Use the presets to jump straight to a regime

Thermal Cloud, Sit at Tc, and Pure Condensate snap Temperature to three characteristic points so you can compare them directly without hunting on the slider.

04

Watch the Time-of-Flight Profile

This is the momentum distribution. A broad hump is thermal atoms; a narrow spike growing out of it is the condensate. Their coexistence — the bimodal shape — is the classic experimental signature of BEC.

05

Adjust Interaction Strength

This widens or narrows the condensate core once it forms, standing in for the atomic scattering length as. It has no effect above Tc, since there's no condensate yet to shape.

06

Read the stat strip and phase bar

T / Tc and Condensate Fraction summarize the state numerically; the phase bar's marker slides along the same thermal → condensate spectrum as the trap chamber colors, and the phase cards below light up to match.
