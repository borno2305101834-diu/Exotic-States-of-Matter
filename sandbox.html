T ≫ Tc

Thermal Gas

Atoms behave as classical billiard balls, spread thin and obeying Maxwell–Boltzmann statistics. Their de Broglie wavelengths are far shorter than the spacing between them, so quantum overlap is negligible.

T ≈ Tc

Quantum Degeneracy

As the cloud cools, matter waves begin to overlap. Phase-space density approaches 1, and a macroscopic population starts piling into the trap's ground state — the first spike appears in the velocity profile.

T ≪ Tc

Bose–Einstein Condensate

A single macroscopic matter wave now describes the whole core. Its shape is no longer set by temperature but by interatomic repulsion — the Thomas–Fermi profile you're steering with the interaction slider.
