Reference

The physics behind the sandbox

An ideal Bose gas in a harmonic trap, plus a mean-field correction for interactions. Simplified enough to run in real time, faithful enough to show the real signatures of condensation.

Critical temperature
Tc = 40 · N1/3 · f  (nK)

The temperature below which condensation begins. It grows with the cube root of atom number N and scales linearly with trap frequency f — a tighter trap confines atoms more, raising the phase-space density at a given temperature.

Condensate fraction
N0 / N = 1 − (T / Tc)3

The fraction of atoms in the ground state, for T below Tc. It rises sharply just below the critical point and approaches 1 as T → 0, tracking the cyan core in the trap chamber.

Sequence

How condensation happens

01

Cool the cloud

Lower the temperature so the atoms move more slowly and their matter waves begin to overlap.

02

Cross Tc

Near the critical temperature, quantum degeneracy becomes important and a narrow central peak begins to appear.

03

Enter the BEC phase

Below Tc, a macroscopic population occupies the lowest quantum state and forms a coherent condensate.

i

What to watch: In the trap, thermal atoms contract into a dense cyan core. In the time-of-flight profile, the condensate produces the narrow central peak of the characteristic bimodal distribution.

!

Model limits: this is a simplified educational model — an ideal-gas Tc combined with a mean-field condensate profile, not a full Gross–Pitaevskii equation solver. It's built to show the right qualitative signatures, not to be quantitatively predictive.
